Two things to get right, restated because they are the crux:
- Column i of the m-matrix is `vec(u_i v_i^T)`, i.e. the entry at row index
  (a * n^2 + b) is `u_i[a] * v_i[b]`, with a and b each ranging over n^2.
- A position is reducible when deleting its column does NOT change the rank.
  Reducible means rank-unchanged, not rank-decreased. Getting this backwards
  makes a correct scheme look reducible, which is the single most likely
  mistake here.
