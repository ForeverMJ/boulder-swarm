# A1 — autonomous exact 4x4x4 scheme at rank <= 47 over F_2 (AlphaTensor parity)

You are the worker for task A1, milestone M8, in a goal-driven multi-agent run.

## The problem, precisely

Construct an exact scheme for multiplying two 4x4 matrices with at most 47 scalar
multiplications, whose arithmetic is exact **over F_2** (coefficients in {0,1},
all equality decisions in F_2 arithmetic). This matches DeepMind's AlphaTensor
flagship record (Nature 610, 47-53, 2022): the first improvement over Strassen's
two-level 49 in that setting, discovered with a TPU-scale search.

**Your target is parity. A landing at 47 is a success. 48/49 are not.**

  triple of 16-bit vectors; equality decisions are XOR masks. Represent the
  64-entry target tensor as a 64-bit bitmap; the rank is over F_2.
- Known structure to lean on: naive(4) = 64 terms; Strassen two-level on 4x4 =
  49; the record is 47 — the gap you close is 2 terms beyond the two-level
